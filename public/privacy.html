隐私说明

本站使用哪些数据

静响榜展示 YouTube 与哔哩哔哩的公开视频及创作者资料，并直接链接至原平台。本站没有账号、站内播放器或广告追踪服务。

浏览器本地记录

收藏、筛选偏好和最近主动打开的视频保存在你的浏览器 LocalStorage 中，不上传到本站。最近访问记录可在收藏页关闭或清空；所有本地数据可导出及导入。清除浏览器数据可能使记录丢失。

第三方平台

打开视频、创作者主页或加载封面时，浏览器会与相应平台建立连接；平台可能按自身政策处理请求。请参阅 YouTube 服务条款及 Google 隐私政策。

Privacy

What this site uses

HUSH INDEX displays public video and creator information from YouTube and Bilibili, with direct links to the original platforms. It has no account system, in-site player or advertising tracker.

Data in your browser

Saved items, filter preferences and videos you choose to open are stored in your browser's LocalStorage. They are not uploaded to this site. You can turn off or clear recent opens on the Saved page, and export or import local data. Clearing browser data may remove these records.

Third-party platforms

Opening a video or creator page, or loading a thumbnail, connects your browser to the relevant platform. That platform may process requests under its own policies. See the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
